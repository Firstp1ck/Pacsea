User behavior

Off

Stop future unattended starts

Pressing Shift+B publishes the off policy before settings I/O. A scan that already owns the start boundary may finish. The next background item stays queued.

On

Reuse consent, never grant it

Enable checks the current disclosure, observation, paid execution, fallback, pricing, and readiness state. Missing prerequisites open guided setup. The toggle never approves them.

Save failure

Remain off for this session

If disabling cannot be saved, runtime authorization stays off. The notice warns that a restart may restore the previous saved value.

Usage

1

Open Pi Scan Overview or Setup

The control is labeled [B] Background scans. Existing lowercase shortcuts do not change.

2

Press Shift+B

When turning scans on, Pacsea either applies the saved policy or opens guided setup to collect missing consent. When turning scans off, an active background scan may finish.

3

Read the top-bar state

Pi BG: ON means configured and ready. OFF means future unattended starts are disabled. OFF · finishing means one active background scan is completing. SETUP means the saved preference is on but required setup is incomplete. … means the runtime is processing a toggle.
